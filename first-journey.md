## The story behind this journey

Where did the journey begin? Why did you choose this place? What happened on the way? What moment do you still remember?

## Photo stories

For each photo, write a specific caption: what is in the frame, what was happening, and why the moment mattered.

## What I would tell another traveller

Include practical advice grounded in your experience. Clearly distinguish personal opinion from facts that need current verification.
